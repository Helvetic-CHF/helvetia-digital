Privacy policy

We handle your data with care and only collect what is needed to process your request. Here is what that means in concrete terms.

Last updated: 21 July 2026

1. Controller

The controller responsible for processing personal data on this website is:

Helvetia Digital
Zurich, Switzerland
Email: helvetiaadigi@gmail.com

2. Principles

We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We only collect data that is required for the respective purpose and do not pass it on to third parties for sale or advertising.

3. Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages – a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you access the website, your browser transmits technically necessary data to GitHub, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page accessed and information about your browser and operating system. This data is required to deliver the page. We ourselves have no access to it and do not analyse it.

4. Fonts (Google Fonts)

We embed fonts from Google Fonts – a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. In doing so, your IP address is transmitted to Google, possibly also to servers in the USA.

The purpose is a consistent and fast display of the page. If you want to avoid this, you can set your browser to block external fonts – the page remains usable but looks different.

5. Appointment booking (Cal.com)

For online appointment booking we use Cal.com – a service of Cal.com, Inc., 2261 Market Street #4382, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA. The booking calendar is embedded directly on our page; this establishes a connection to Cal.com servers.

When you book an appointment, the following data is collected and transmitted to Cal.com:

  • Your name and email address
  • Date, time and time zone of the requested appointment
  • Voluntary information you provide in the booking form

This data is used exclusively to arrange and confirm the appointment. The appointment is also transferred to our Google Calendar (Google Ireland Limited) so that we can manage it. If the meeting takes place by video, the Cal Video service is used for this.

The legal basis is the implementation of pre-contractual measures at your request. You can cancel an appointment at any time via the link in your confirmation email.

As Cal.com and Google may also process data in the USA, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses of the EU Commission and the corresponding guarantees of the providers.

6. Contact form

The contact form on this website does not send your details to a server of ours. When you submit it, your own email program opens with a prepared message that you send yourself. So no processing by us takes place before you send the email.

7. Contacting us by email

If you write to us, we process your details in order to answer your request. Our email inbox is hosted by Google (Gmail). We keep enquiries for as long as necessary to process them and for any follow-up questions, and delete them afterwards unless statutory retention obligations apply.

8. Cookies and analytics

We use no cookies of our own, no tracking and no analytics tools. The embedded Cal.com booking calendar may use technically necessary cookies for its operation.

9. Your rights

Within the scope of applicable law, you have in particular the right to information about the data processed about you, to correction of inaccurate data, to deletion, to restriction of processing and to object. To exercise these rights, simply contact helvetiaadigi@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Switzerland with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

10. Data security

This website is delivered exclusively in encrypted form via HTTPS. We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access.

11. Changes

We may amend this privacy policy if our services or the legal requirements change. The version published on this page at any given time applies.
