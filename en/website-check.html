Checkpoints

What we check for you

  1. Mobile display

    We check whether your website displays properly on a smartphone and is easy to use.

  2. Loading time

    We measure how fast your page loads and show what slows it down.

  3. Google discoverability

    We look at whether your business is found on Google and whether the key basics are in place.

  4. Contact and booking

    We check whether prospects can call you, write to you or book an appointment in just a few clicks.

    We check whether an imprint and privacy policy exist and meet the requirements of the Swiss FADP and the GDPR.

Good to know

What does it really cost?
Nothing. The website check is free, and you receive the written assessment with nothing in return. We invest this time because it is the best way to show how we work.
Do I have to buy anything afterwards?
No. The assessment is without obligation, and no sales conversation is needed. If you want to implement the recommendations with us, we are glad – but you can just as well tackle them yourself or with another provider.
What is in the assessment?
A short assessment of each of the five checkpoints: mobile display, loading time, Google discoverability, contact and booking, and legal. For each point you get concrete recommendations on what you can improve.
