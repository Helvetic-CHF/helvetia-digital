Frequently asked questions

Good to know

How do instalments work?
You pay the project price in six equal monthly instalments – no bank, no credit check, directly to us. The first instalment is due when the project starts. With full payment you benefit from the best price.
How long does a website project take?
Depending on the scope, usually two to four weeks from the initial consultation – depending on how quickly content such as copy and images is available. We set the exact schedule together at the start.
What do I need to get started?
Just an idea of what your presence should achieve. A logo, copy and images are helpful but not a must – we support you with structure, wording and image selection.
What happens after go-live?
Adjustments and support are included in the first month. After that we continue to look after your website on request – from individual changes to ongoing maintenance of content and Google profile.
Are there any running costs?
Operating the site involves the usual costs for domain and hosting (usually less than CHF 15.– per month, depending on the provider). We advise you transparently and do not tie you to a contract of our own.
